# Product Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Design
- **Core Aesthetic:** STRICT "Gothic-Fantasy" theme based on *Anima: Beyond Fantasy*.
- **Mood:** Dark, dramatic, serious, and occult. Avoid "cute", "chibi", or "happy" vibes typically associated with vanilla Ragnarok Online.
- **Color Palette:**
    - **Primary:** Obsidian Black / Deep Charcoal.
    - **Accent:** Antique Gold / Brass / Crimson Red.
    - **Magic/Tech Elements:** "Zeon" Green or Arcane Blue (glow effects).
- **Style References:** Detailed 90s Anime style (e.g., *Record of Lodoss War*, *Vampire Hunter D*), intricate occult symbols (Circles of Zeon), and heavy ornamentation.
- **Prohibited:** Flat corporate design, generic "cute" anime styles, bright/pastel color schemes.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Immersion First:** UI elements should feel like diegetic parts of the world (e.g., spellbooks, scrolls, ancient machinery) rather than generic overlays.
- **Complexity Management:** While the game is complex, the interface should organize information hierarchically to prevent cognitive overload without simplifying the deep mechanics.
- **Responsiveness:** Inputs must be immediate and snappy. Animations should be fluid but never impede gameplay speed.
- **Customizability:** Allow power users to move, resize, and toggle almost every UI element to suit their specific playstyle (Support/DPS/Tank).

## Tone of Voice
- **Narrative:** Archaic, grandiose, and slightly foreboding. Use terms like "Grimoire" instead of "Settings" or "Commune" instead of "Chat".
- **System Messages:** Clear and concise, but styled to fit the theme (e.g., "Connection Severed" rather than "Disconnected").

## Accessibility
- **Contrast:** Ensure high contrast between text (Gold/White) and backgrounds (Black/Dark Grey) for readability despite the dark theme.
- **Scaling:** UI scaling support is mandatory for high-DPI displays.
- **Input:** Full keyboard remapping and controller support considerations.
